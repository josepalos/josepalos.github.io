Hi!

I am a researcher in the University of Lleida. My research work is about artificial intelligence and automated reasoning, with a focus on Boolean satisfiability (SAT), MaxSAT, and combinatorial optimization. My research explores the development and configuration of efficient solving techniques, as well as the exploration of different ways to encode problems, mainly to SAT/MaxSAT. For an overview of my research, see my publications and ORCID profile.
