Reductions and gadgets are key tools in computational complexity, with gadgets serving as the
building blocks of reductions. Despite their importance, gadget construction has traditionally been
ad hoc. We present a method to compute gadgets automatically, enabling systematic reductions
and offering insights for both theory and practical solver design.
